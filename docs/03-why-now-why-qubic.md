# 03 — Why now, why Qubic

## Why Qubic is structurally important (not a cosmetic chain choice)

1. **The supply already exists.** Qubic's mining network is a large, already-incentivized fleet of modern CPUs. Computor operators are moving to AVX-512 hardware, and mining guides recommend high-end Ryzen, Threadripper and EPYC chips. QCX does not need to bootstrap supply the way other compute marketplaces do.

2. **Pools already switch workloads.** Qubic pools have run more than one workload on the same miners: AI training plus XMR mining in the past, and DOGE mining today. QCX adds one more paid workload to infrastructure that already exists.

3. **The revenue loop already exists.** Qubic converts external mining revenue into QUBIC buybacks, miner payouts and burns. QCX plugs a new revenue source into that proven loop.

4. **Feeless settlement.** One job can split into thousands of tasks. Paying each miner per task is uneconomic on chains with fees. On Qubic it costs nothing.

5. **Public auditability.** Each job's hash, result hash, buyback amount and burn are written to Qubic, so anyone can check that client revenue actually reached the token.

6. **It matches Qubic's roadmap.** Qubic's own materials list "Outsourced Computations" (climate models, protein folding) as a future direction. QCX makes that direction a product with paying customers.

## Measurable Qubic adoption QCX should create

| Metric | Target 6 months after launch (hypothesis) |
|---|---|
| External revenue converted into QUBIC buybacks | Published monthly; target to be set after the M2 pilot |
| QUBIC burned from QCX surplus | Published monthly, verifiable on-chain |
| Miners earning from QCX jobs | 200+ |
| New paying clients who have never used crypto | 20+ |

## Why now

- Qubic moved its external mining from XMR to DOGE, which showed that pools and miners can adopt a new paid workload. Compute for real clients is the next step, without the reputational cost of attacking or depending on another chain.
- After recent delistings and the October wallet incident, Qubic needs revenue that is visible, external and verifiable. QCX produces exactly that.
